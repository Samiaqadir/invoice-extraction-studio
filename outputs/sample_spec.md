## 3. Schema & Validation Specifications

| Field Name | Type | Optionality | Validation & Constraints | Mathematical Consistency Invariant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `vendor_name` | `string` | **Required** | `min_length=1`, leading/trailing whitespace stripped. | Must not be empty. |
| `invoice_date` | `string` | Optional | ISO format `YYYY-MM-DD` or text date. `null` if omitted. | Issued date of the invoice. |
| `due_date` | `string` | Optional | ISO format `YYYY-MM-DD` or text date. `null` if omitted. | Payment due date. |
| `line_items` | `List[LineItem]` | **Required** | List of line items. Minimum 0 items. | `sum(item.total) == subtotal` within ±$0.02 tolerance. |
| `line_items[].description` | `string` | **Required** | `min_length=1`, non-empty product or service name. | Identifies billed service. |
| `line_items[].quantity` | `float` | **Required** | `gt=0.0`. Numerical billed quantity. | Multiplicand in line item product. |
| `line_items[].unit_price` | `float` | **Required** | `ge=0.0`. Price per individual unit. | Multiplier in line item product. |
| `line_items[].total` | `float` | **Required** | `ge=0.0`. Total charge for line item. | `quantity * unit_price == total` within ±$0.02. |
| `subtotal` | `float` | Optional | `ge=0.0`. Net amount before taxes. `null` if absent. | Sum of line items before tax. |
| `tax_amount` | `float` | Optional | `ge=0.0`. Tax / VAT / GST. `null` if tax exempt. | Applied tax. |
| `grand_total` | `float` | **Required** | `ge=0.0`. Total payable amount. | `round(subtotal + (tax_amount or 0), 2) == round(grand_total, 2)`. |

## 4. Discrepancy & Defect Taxonomy Mapping

When extraction diverges from benchmark ground truth, defects are logged into one of 4 canonical categories:

1. **`field_missing`**: A required field (`vendor_name`, `grand_total`) or line item was omitted or returned null.
2. **`ocr_misrecognition`**: Text or numbers have character confusion or typographical errors (e.g., `"2026-10-15"` recognized as `"2026-10-16"`).
3. **`decimal_misalignment`**: Number has correct digit sequence but decimal point shifted by power of 10 (e.g., `$45.00` extracted as `$450.00`).
4. **`mathematical_discrepancy`**: Arithmetic inconsistency detected (e.g., `quantity * unit_price != total` or `subtotal + tax != grand_total`).
